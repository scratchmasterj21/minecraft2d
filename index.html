Controls

1–9 Select item

Click Place or mine

WASD Move / jump

E Open / close inventory

E + hover Open / close chest, crafting table, door

Space Drop single tile from a stack while dragging

F Eat food

N Label a sign or chest

Q Drop item

P Pause / unpause

T Talk / command

O Save your game

M Music / sounds

Shift Sprint

Saving your world

Signed in with your school Google account.

This game does not save to the cloud by itself. Use your Google account to keep the save string somewhere you can open later (Google Docs, Drive, or Classroom).

  1. Press O in the game. Double-click the long number in the list, then copy it (Ctrl+C on Windows, ⌘C on Mac).
  2. Paste it into your save Doc (below or in a tab you opened from the button).
  3. Next time: choose Load Game on the title screen, paste the whole string (Ctrl+V / ⌘V), and confirm.
